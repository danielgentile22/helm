Helm 2 · visual direction

Amber Rail

One hairline rail runs the full height of every thread. Prompts, thinking, activity and output all hang off that single line, so a thread reads as one unbroken terminal session rather than a stack of cards. The rail itself is the live indicator: it carries a travelling amber light while a turn is running. Amber against warm ink owns the hue; indigo is the second signal axis, chosen because amber and blue stay distinguishable under red-green colour blindness.

9 phone frames + 1 laptop frame · no network, no web fonts
