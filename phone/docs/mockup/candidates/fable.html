Amber Gutter

One hue, one structural device. The whole log is a single ruled column with a 28 px gutter on the left that carries a glyph for every kind of line, exactly the way a terminal prompt margin does. Amber phosphor on warm black in the dark, iron-gall ink on paper in the light. Nothing in the app is ever red or green.
